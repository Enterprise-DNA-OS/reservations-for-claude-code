# Record checks and their sources

Checked 29 September 2026. The command identifies records to review. It does not certify legal compliance, verify a dish or replace the kitchen's controls.

| Check | Record rule | Source and scope |
|---|---|---|
| ALLERGEN-HANDOVER | Upcoming active bookings with a recorded allergy but no named kitchen acknowledgement are listed. | [FSANZ food allergen portal](https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/foodallergies/food-allergen-portal) describes the food service responsibility to help a customer who discloses an allergy with menu choices. A named handover is our operational control, not a statutory form. |
| RETENTION-REVIEW | Guest retention_review_on before today requires a purpose review. | [NZ Privacy Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) limits retention to a lawful purpose. [OAIC APP 11](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information) addresses destruction or de-identification when information is no longer needed, for entities covered by that Act, subject to exceptions. |

The default annual review is a house policy, not a legal retention period. Confirm which rules apply to the business and any required retention before deletion. The command never deletes a guest. Record the purpose and next review with retention-review. Marketing consent is not inferred from a reservation; this version never sends campaigns.

Kitchen staff must check ingredients, preparation and cross-contact controls directly. Acknowledgement records who received the note; it does not say the meal is safe. Contact details and dietary notes stay in authorised internal briefings. Limit database access and protect backups.
